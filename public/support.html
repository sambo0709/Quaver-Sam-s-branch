QUAVER HELP

How can we help?

Quick answers for accounts, recommendations, playlists, and Spotify playback.

Spotify will not connect

Confirm you are logged into Quaver, allow the requested Spotify permissions, and make sure pop-ups or redirects are not blocked. You can reconnect from Settings.

Spotify settings →

A song will not play

Full in-app playback requires an eligible Spotify account. When a preview exists, Quaver can use it as a fallback; otherwise open the track in Spotify.

Return to your mix →

My recommendations feel off

Use the feedback actions on songs, adjust recommendation variety, and update loved or blocked artists. Quaver uses those signals on future mixes.

Update your taste →

Manage or delete my data

You can clear listening and mood history, reset recommendation feedback, download your information, or delete your account.

Privacy settings →

Playlist sharing

Shared playlists are visible to anyone with the link and can display your Quaver name. Turn sharing off from the playlist library whenever you want.

Open playlists →

Still need help?

A monitored support address must be connected before public registration opens. Until then, keep diagnostic details such as the page, browser, and exact error message.
