Privacy Policy

Last updated September 8, 2026

What Quaver stores

When you create an account, Quaver stores your display name, email address, secured password hash, preferences, playlists, mood check-ins, listening history, and recommendation feedback. If you connect Spotify, Quaver stores your Spotify identity and an encrypted authorization refresh token.

How your data is used

Quaver uses this information to operate your account, create and improve mood-based recommendations, remember your preferences, maintain playlists, provide playback, and diagnose service failures. Quaver does not sell personal information.

Spotify

Music search, metadata, playback, and exports are supplied through Spotify. Spotify's own terms and privacy policy also apply when you connect an account or open Spotify content.

Sharing

Playlists are private unless you explicitly enable sharing. A shared playlist can be viewed by anyone who has its link and may show your Quaver display name.

Your choices

Settings lets you clear mood history, listening history, and recommendation feedback; disconnect Spotify; download your account data; or permanently delete your account.

Retention and security

Data remains while your account is active unless you clear or delete it. Quaver uses encrypted transport in production, restricted cookies, access controls, and encrypted Spotify refresh tokens. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.
